# NUDGE — AI Product Studio for Asian SMEs

NUDGE builds practical, lightweight AI tools for Indian and Asian SMEs. Most AI products today target large enterprises or Western-style businesses, while local SMEs still sell through physical stores, WhatsApp, Instagram, and simple ecommerce sites. We build AI that fits how these businesses already work, instead of forcing them into complex software.

**Current focus:** [Nudge Agent](https://nudgeagent.app), a WhatsApp-native AI Front Desk for SMEs.
**First product:** [TrialRoom Studio](https://trialroomstudio.com/), a virtual try-on platform — now validated with 4–5 paying clients.

This page is the official progress and reporting page for our SEP project under Innovation Lab @ CCDS. It documents direction, progress, videos, financials, competitions, and status. Some production code is private (client pilots and proprietary work), so this repo focuses on documentation.

## Journey

- **Pivoted** to NUDGE, an AI product studio for Asian SMEs, after customer discovery showed local SMEs are underserved by current AI software (too expensive, too enterprise, not built for how they sell).

- **Now focused on Nudge Agent** — a WhatsApp-native AI Front Desk — after seeing that SMEs across India and SEA run their whole customer relationship on WhatsApp and are drowning in it.

## Current Product: Nudge Agent

[nudgeagent.app](https://nudgeagent.app) — a **WhatsApp-native AI Front Desk** for booking-driven SMEs. Instead of another dashboard to learn, Nudge Agent runs a business's WhatsApp for them.

**The problem.** WhatsApp is where customers are, but running it is a second job: Meta's Business API is a compliance maze, marketing needs approved templates and opt-in lists, and leads die waiting (a lead messages at 11 pm, gets a reply at noon, and has already booked elsewhere). Speed-to-lead kills revenue.

**The solution — a three-step ladder:**
1. **Compliance, done** — concierge Meta onboarding: verification, API connection, green tick.
2. **Marketing, done** — product photo in, compliant campaign out to opted-in contacts, with delivery/reads/clicks tracked.
3. **Replies, done (the core)** — an AI Front Desk trained on the business that **answers** in seconds, **books** into the real calendar, **chases** quiet leads, **collects** deposits, and hands off to the team when needed.

**Built on** the official Meta Cloud API, with Stripe / PayNow / GrabPay / WhatsApp Pay support and 282 automated tests.

**Target market.** High-inquiry, high-ticket, booking-driven businesses where the front desk is the bottleneck: hair and aesthetic clinics, dental, IVF, real estate, restaurants, and salons. Beachhead: high-ticket clinics (paid-ad leads decay within the hour; no-shows run 15–40%). Singapore first, then SEA.

## First Product: TrialRoom Studio (Validated)

[trialroomstudio.com](https://trialroomstudio.com/) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trialroomstudio.app)

A virtual try-on platform for fashion retailers, boutiques, fabric shops, and ecommerce brands, letting customers see how a garment or fabric looks on them before buying. It shipped as both an **in-shop retailer app** and an embeddable **"Try It On"** website widget for ecommerce.

**Outcome:** moved from pilots to real revenue with **4–5 paying clients**, validating the NUDGE studio model. Those learnings now carry into Nudge Agent, our current focus.
