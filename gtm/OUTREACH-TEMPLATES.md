# Outreach & Demo Templates — Nudge Reach

_Copy-paste assets. Keep them personal — swap the [brackets]. Short, warm, no jargon. Hindi/Hinglish variants included; use whichever fits the person._

---

## 1. Instagram DM — cold, to a small product seller

**English**
> Hi [name] 👋 Love your [sarees / pieces] — that [specific recent post, e.g. "red Banarasi from last week"] is gorgeous.
> Quick one: I'm building a tool for shops like yours that turns a single product photo into a ready-to-send WhatsApp campaign for your customers — takes about a minute, no design or typing.
> Can I show you a 60-sec demo using one of your own products? Free, no catch — I just want feedback from real shop owners.

**Hinglish**
> Hi [name] 👋 Aapki [sarees] bahut sundar hain — woh [specific item] post wala piece toh kamaal ka tha.
> Main ek tool bana raha hoon jo ek product photo se seedha WhatsApp campaign bana deta hai jo aap apne customers ko bhej sakein — bas 1 minute, na design na typing.
> Aapke hi kisi product se 1-minute demo dikha sakta hoon? Bilkul free — sirf aapka feedback chahiye.

## 3. In-person opener (market visit)

> "Bhaiya/Didi, ek minute — aap apne customers ko naye products ke baare mein WhatsApp pe batate ho? … Main dikhata hoon, aapke is [product] ki photo leta hoon …" → *take photo, run the live demo, hand them the phone.*

The product does the talking. Don't explain features — show the output and watch their reaction.

---

## 4. The 60-second demo script (in person or on a call)

1. **Set up (5s):** "I'll use one of your actual products so it's real."
2. **Photo (10s):** Take a clean photo of one product on your phone, in the live app.
3. **Generate (15s):** Let it produce the campaign. Stay quiet while it runs.
4. **Reveal (20s):** Show the WhatsApp-style preview — headline, message with their customer's name filled in, the offer, the opt-out. "This is exactly what your customer sees."
5. **Cost (5s):** "Sending to 200 customers costs about ₹[X] — you see the estimate before you send."
6. **Ask (5s):** "Want me to set this up for your shop? First campaign's free."

---

## 5. Demo Reel outline (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)

- **Hook (0–3s):** On-screen text — "Marketing for your shop in 60 seconds 👇" over a hand photographing a product.
- **0–15s:** Real-time: photograph a saree → tap generate.
- **15–35s:** Screen-record the finished WhatsApp campaign appearing, name auto-filled.
- **35–50s:** "No typing. No designer. Stays WhatsApp-compliant automatically."
- **50–60s:** CTA — "Comment SHOP and I'll set you up free" / link in bio to the waitlist.

Make 5–10 of these with different products/verticals. The wedge is inherently visual — this is your cheapest, highest-signal channel.

## 6. Reseller / agency pitch (for the channel motion)

> Hi [name] — you set up WhatsApp Business for shops, right? I've built a tool that lets those shops create + send campaigns from just a product photo. If you onboard shops onto it, I'll give you [20–30%] recurring revenue share on every one that stays active. You become their "WhatsApp marketing person" without doing the manual work each time. Want to try it with one or two of your clients?

---

## 7. Referral ask (to a happy shop, at the moment of delight)

> So glad that campaign got responses! 🙌 If you know other shop owners who'd want this, introduce me and you both get a free month when they start. Who comes to mind?

Trigger this **right after** a campaign gets good replies — not at random.
