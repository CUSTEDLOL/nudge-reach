# Go-To-Market & Distribution Plan — Nudge Reach

_Companion to the discovery playbook. This is the "once validated, how do we get to the first 100 paying shops" plan. Sequence matters: don't run paid acquisition before the manual motion works._

---

## 1. The core insight

WhatsApp marketing tools mostly acquire customers the same way: paid ads + SEO + reseller channels, selling a *dashboard*. Nudge's distribution advantage should mirror its product advantage — **be where non-technical shop owners already are, and let the product demo itself in 60 seconds** (photo → campaign is the best sales pitch there is).

Distribution priority for a pre-PMF founder: **founder-led, hands-on, high-touch first. Automated/paid later.**

---

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (commercial)

Same as discovery ICP, sharpened:
- Owner-operated visual-product retailer (apparel/textiles, jewellery, décor, gifting, bakery).
- Has an existing informal customer list (≥50 contacts).
- Posts product photos already.
- Tier-1/Tier-2 India, English or Hindi comfortable.
- Pain: "I want repeat customers to come back, but marketing is a hassle."

One ICP, one vertical, one city to start. Recommend **apparel/textiles** (matches the demo) in **your home city**.

---

## 3. Channels (ranked, with the sequence to run them)

### Phase A — Manual, founder-led (shops 1–20)
1. **Warm network + in-person market visits.** Walk the market with the phone demo. Highest conversion, zero CAC, fastest learning. This is the whole game early on.
2. **Instagram DMs to small product sellers.** Personalized, references their actual posts. (Templates in OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md.)
3. **"Done-for-you" onboarding.** For the first cohort, *you* set up their account, import their contacts, and send their first campaign with them. Removes 100% of friction and teaches you the onboarding gaps.

### Phase B — Repeatable, semi-scaled (shops 20–100)
4. **Referrals as a built-in loop.** Every happy shop knows 5 others in the same market. Add a referral incentive (1 free month per referred shop that activates). Ask at the moment of delight — right after a campaign gets good responses.
5. **Reseller / agency channel.** Freelancers and small agencies already set up WhatsApp Business + Meta for shops. Recruit a handful as resellers: they onboard shops, you give a revenue share (e.g., 20–30% recurring). This is how Indian SaaS reaches the long tail. Doubles as discovery-intro source.
6. **Local creators & market associations.** Micro-influencers in the retail-tips niche, traders' association WhatsApp groups, a short demo reel.
7. **Content: the demo *is* the content.** Short vertical videos: "Sells a saree → finished WhatsApp campaign in 60 seconds." Post on Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts. This format is the cheapest proof of the wedge.

### Phase C — Paid, only after the above converts (post-PMF)
8. **Click-to-WhatsApp ads / Meta ads** to a demo. Only once you know CAC < LTV from organic. (Note: this is AiSensy's home turf — don't fight them here early.)

## 4. Pricing (starting hypothesis — validate in discovery)

Keep it stupidly simple, INR-native, transparent (directly attacking AiSensy's "surprise billing" complaint).

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Free trial** | ₹0 — first campaign free, up to ~50 sends (simulation or real) | Remove all friction to first value |
| **Starter** | **₹499/mo** + pass-through message cost | Solo shop, occasional campaigns |
| **Growth** | **₹1,299/mo** + message cost | Regular weekly campaigns, multiple audiences |

Principles:
- **Message costs passed through transparently** (show the Meta rate ~₹0.86 marketing / ₹0.115 utility; you already surface an INR estimate in-app). No markup games early — trust is the wedge vs. AiSensy.
- Undercut AiSensy/Interakt's effective entry (~₹999–2,499/mo) on the *platform fee* while being radically simpler.
- Annual option later. Don't overthink pricing pre-PMF — the goal of early pricing is to test willingness-to-pay, not to maximize revenue.

## 5. First-100 plan (concrete)

- **Week 1–3:** Discovery sprint (12 interviews) + onboard 3–5 committed shops by hand. Goal: one shop sends a real campaign and reports a customer response.
- **Week 4–6:** Tighten onboarding to <10 min self-serve. Get to ~20 shops via in-person + IG DMs + referrals from the first cohort. Capture 2–3 testimonial videos.
- **Week 7–12:** Stand up the referral loop + recruit 2–3 resellers. Publish demo Reels weekly. Target 100 shops, ≥20 paying.

**North-star metric early:** number of shops that have **sent a real campaign and gotten a reply** (activation), not signups.

## 7. What success looks like at each gate

- **Gate 1 (validation):** 3+ shops verbally commit / pre-pay. → build distribution.
- **Gate 2 (activation):** 1 shop sends a real campaign and gets customer replies. → this is the proof everything hinges on; it also requires going live on real WhatsApp (see FIRST-RETAILER-ONBOARDING.md).
- **Gate 3 (repeatability):** 10 shops onboarded via a channel you didn't personally hustle (referral/reseller/inbound). → you have a distribution engine.
