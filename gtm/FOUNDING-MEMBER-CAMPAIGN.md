# Founding Member Campaign — "First 100 Shops"

_The India-friendly alternative to Kickstarter. Kickstarter can't be launched from India and is a poor fit for a B2B SaaS subscription (it's built for creative/hardware projects, and pledge "rewards" that are really subscriptions run into its financial-incentive rules). This campaign achieves the same goals — early commitment, cash, momentum, social proof — without any platform gatekeeping, on channels your customers already use._

---

## What we're really after (the Kickstarter goals, restated)

1. **Validated demand with money attached** — people paying before the product is fully proven.
2. **A launch moment** — scarcity + deadline that creates urgency and word of mouth.
3. **Social proof** — a public "X of 100 shops joined" counter.
4. **A founding community** — first users who feel ownership and refer others.

A founding-member pre-sale delivers all four, and the buyers are the exact people we want (real shop owners), not Kickstarter's hardware-backer crowd.

---

## The offer — "Founding 100"

> **The first 100 shops to join lock in founding-member pricing for life and get hands-on setup from the founder.**

| | Founding Member | Regular (later) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | **₹299/mo for life** (or ₹2,499/year) | ₹499–1,299/mo |
| First campaign | Free, set up *with you* personally | Self-serve |
| "Founding Shop" badge | Yes — listed on site (opt-in) | — |
| Direct line to founder | Yes (WhatsApp) | Support queue |
| Vote on the roadmap | Yes | — |
| Price increases | Never, as long as you stay | Standard pricing applies |

**Why a small monthly, not a big pledge:** Indian SME owners commit more readily to ₹299/mo than a large upfront pledge, and a paid subscription is the truest validation signal. Optionally offer an annual prepay (₹2,499) for those who want the "back the project" feeling — that's your upfront cash.

**Commitment ladder (so no one bounces at "pay now"):**
1. **Join waitlist** (free) — the landing page.
2. **Reserve a founding spot** — ₹0 now, or a small refundable ₹99 deposit to "hold your spot in the first 100."
3. **Activate** — first campaign set up with you; convert to the ₹299/mo founding plan.

Track the funnel: waitlist → reserved → activated → paying.

## 60–90 second video script (founder-shot, phone is fine)

1. **(0–8s) Hook — founder to camera, in a real shop if possible:** "If you run a shop, you've got something new to sell every week. Telling your customers shouldn't take an hour. Watch this."
2. **(8–30s) Live demo:** Photograph a real product → tap generate → the finished WhatsApp campaign appears, name auto-filled. Narrate: "One photo. That's it. The message, the offer, the buttons — done. And it stays within WhatsApp's rules."
3. **(30–45s) The cost line:** Show the ₹ estimate. "You see what it costs before you send. No surprises."
4. **(45–70s) The ask:** "We're taking our first 100 shops and setting each one up personally. Founding shops pay ₹299 a month — for life. That price never goes up for you."
5. **(70–90s) CTA:** "Tap the link, tell us about your shop, and we'll WhatsApp you to set it up — your first campaign is free. Be one of the first 100."

Shoot 3 variants for different verticals (saree shop, jeweller, bakery). Vertical video, captions burned in (many will watch muted).

## Launch sequence (2 weeks)

**Pre-launch (week 0):**
- Landing page live with the "X / 100 joined" counter.
- 5–10 demo Reels queued.
- 20–30 warm prospects lined up from discovery to seed day-1 signups (never launch to silence).

**Launch day:**
- Post the main video everywhere at once: Instagram, WhatsApp Status, local business groups, your network.
- Personally WhatsApp every warm prospect with the offer (templates in OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md).
- Goal: 10–15 reservations day one so the counter shows movement.

**Days 2–10:**
- One demo Reel/day, each a different product.
- Post the counter as it climbs ("63 of 100 spots left") — scarcity is the engine.
- DM small product sellers daily.
- Activate reserved shops (set up their first campaign) — each activation = a testimonial + referral ask.

**Days 11–14 — the close:**
- "Final spots" push. Public countdown.
- When 100 fill (or the deadline hits), close founding pricing publicly. The close *is* the next campaign's hook ("Founding 100 sold out — join the waitlist for the next cohort").

## FAQ (pre-empt the objections)

- **Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?** No to try it (we run in simulation first). To send to real customers we help you set up the official WhatsApp Business API — we walk you through it.
- **Is this allowed / will I get blocked?** We only message customers who opted in, every message includes an opt-out, and campaigns are kept within WhatsApp's rules automatically. That's the whole point.
- **What does sending cost?** A small platform fee (₹299/mo founding) plus WhatsApp's own per-message charge (~₹0.86 per marketing message in India). You always see the total estimate before you send.
- **What if it doesn't work for me?** Your first campaign is free and we set it up with you. Annual prepay is refundable in the first 30 days.
- **Why only 100 shops?** We set up each shop personally so it actually works for you. Founding pricing is our thank-you for trusting us early.

## Success metrics

- **Primary:** number of shops that **activate** (send a real or simulated first campaign), not just reserve.
- 100 reservations → 40+ activations → 25+ paying founding members would be a strong outcome and a real PMF signal.
- Each activated shop should generate ≥1 referral ask and ideally 1 testimonial clip.
